CalInv privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

CalInv is an offline stock and sales app. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data lives.

What the app stores

  • What you enter: store name, products, prices, costs, currencies and exchange rates, stock entries, losses, sales, payments and returns.
  • Product photos you take with the camera or pick from your gallery.

Where it lives

Everything is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. CalInv has no servers, creates no accounts and sends your data to no one. It uses no ads, no analytics and no third-party services that collect information.

Backups

When you choose "Create and share backup", the app builds a file with your data and photos and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where to save it or whom to send it to; from then on, that file is under the control of the service you chose.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph products when you ask.
  • Photos and files: only to pick a product image or a backup file when you ask.

Deleting your data

You can delete products and archive what you no longer use inside the app. To delete everything, uninstall CalInv: the system removes all of its data from the phone. Backups you saved outside the app are not deleted automatically.

Children

CalInv is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with its update date.
