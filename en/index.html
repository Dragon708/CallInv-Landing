Features

Everything your shop needs, nothing it doesn't.

From receiving stock to giving change, CalInv keeps the books for you.

Stock that adds up

  • Products by unit, weight or volume, with variants (sizes, flavors) and presentations (single, 6-pack, box).
  • Stock entries by lot with cost, currency and expiry date.
  • Losses, damaged or expired goods, and physical counts that adjust stock for you.
  • Low-stock and expiry warnings.
  • Full movement history per product.

Sell in a few taps

  • Search or tap a product and it is in the cart.
  • Editable price, discounts and a warning when stock runs short.
  • Payment in several currencies with your own exchange rates and change calculation.
  • Full or partial returns that go back to the original lot.

Real profit

  • Sales, gross profit, losses, net profit and margin for today, the week or the month.
  • Sales by hour and by day, and your best sellers.
  • The cost of each sale comes from the lots it used, not an average.

Several businesses

  • Keep more than one store on the same phone, each with its own data.
  • Available in Spanish and English, with light and dark themes.
